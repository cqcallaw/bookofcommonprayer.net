Preferences

A note about the preferences: Preferences are stored for 30 days using cookies, but which are renewed on a rolling basis each time you visit the site so that your preference expiration will always renew to 30 days from your last visit.

Daily Office Preferences

These preferences determine what the system will pull up for you when you have selected today's office from the menu. The default settings have been pre-selected for you.

If you normally do only one prayer per day (e.g. Morning Prayer), select All readings here. By default today's office will switch over to Evening Prayer after 12:00 in your selected timezone, and provide you with the readings appointed for Evening Prayer. Selecting "All" here will make today's office use your choice for prayer according to your selected rite. Choose BCP if you want the Psalms from the BCP Psalter, or Bible if you want the psalms in your preferred bible version.

 





 

Lectionary Preferences

These preferences determine whether the readings will come from the 1979 Book of Common Prayer, or from the Revised Common Lectionary, and whether to use a traditional or contemporary collect and preface. Choose BCP if you want the Psalms from the BCP Psalter, or Bible if you want the psalms in your preferred bible version.
